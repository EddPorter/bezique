# Logo prompt

A brief for an image-generating or design LLM. Paste the prompt below as it stands.

## Prompt

Design a logo for "Bezique", a browser-based card game of the classic French trick-taking family (a sibling of Pinochle). The audience is card-game enthusiasts and players of traditional games; the tone is refined, warm and slightly old-world, not casino-flashy or childish.

**Concept.** The defining meld of the game is the "Bezique": the queen of spades paired with the jack of diamonds. Build the mark around that pairing: a stylised spade and a diamond overlapping or interlocking, or a queen and a jack card fanned together. Keep it abstract enough to read as a symbol rather than an illustration of two specific cards. Do not use a full, realistic card face.

**Style.** Flat vector, geometric, with confident simple shapes and at most three colours. Slight art-deco or vintage playing-card character. No gradients, no drop shadows, no photographic textures, no 3D bevels.

**Palette.** Deep baize green (#0f4029) as the background or primary colour, cream (#f4ecd8) for the spade and any text, and a muted red (#c9433c) for the diamond. These match the existing favicon and game table.

**Typography.** If a wordmark is included, set "Bezique" in a high-contrast serif (Georgia-like, with a Didone or old-style feel), in cream on green, with generous letter-spacing. The wordmark is optional; the symbol must work alone.

**Deliverables.**
1. A square symbol-only mark that holds up at 16 × 16 px as a favicon and at 512 × 512 px as an app or social icon, with a rounded-square background (corner radius about 22%).
2. A horizontal lockup of the symbol beside the wordmark.
3. Each as clean SVG with minimal paths, no embedded raster images, no text-to-outline artefacts, and a transparent background variant.

**Avoid.** Poker chips, dice, casino imagery, crowns, clichéd ace-of-spades silhouettes, hearts or clubs (the game's identity is spade and diamond), any text other than "Bezique", and anything resembling the logo of an existing card-game brand.

**Variations to offer.** Provide three distinct concepts: (a) interlocking spade and diamond, (b) two overlapping card corners showing the queen's spade and the jack's diamond pips, (c) a monogram "B" whose counters are formed by a spade and a diamond.
